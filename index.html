How this plan works

  • Meals. Your 28-day rotation follows the calendar. Cycle day 1 was 27 Oct, so 10 Nov is cycle day 15. The rotation assumes a 28-day cycle. If your cycle runs longer or shorter, tell me and I will re-anchor the phase labels.
  • Calories. Weeks 1 and 2 sit at maintenance. From Week 3 the target is 350 kcal below maintenance, with the travel weeks left out. From Week 18 you eat at maintenance because the long walks need the fuel. Weights and macros are projections from your July scans, so re-set them after your Week 6 scan.
  • Sessions. Leave 48 to 72 hours between strength days. Your 60-minute session is Wednesday step-ups, one per week at most. Do not eat back the training burn, which is roughly 120 to 170 kcal for 30 minutes and 250 to 350 kcal for 60 minutes (estimates).
  • Deloads. Weeks 4, 6, 13, 17 and 21 are deload or light weeks, and Weeks 24 and 25 are the taper. Keep them. Your recovery is slower than average and your stress fracture risk is elevated.
  • Progress rule. If you cannot finish a session comfortably, or you feel pain in one spot on a shin or foot that worsens with impact, repeat the week instead of moving on and see a doctor about the pain.
  • The climb. The route from Timpohon Gate is about 6 km on day 1 to Laban Rata (roughly 4 to 5 hours), then a summit start near 2 am and a long descent on day 2. The summit is 4,095 m. I estimate about 2,200 m of climbing and the same of descent, so the step-up and descent work matters more than flat mileage.
  • Altitude. Fitness does not stop altitude sickness. Spend a night at Park HQ (1,500 m) before climbing, move slowly and tell your guide about headache, dizziness or nausea. Get your blood pressure checked and speak to a doctor before the trip, because high blood pressure is one of your genetic flags.
  • Hike-day food. Long days burn more than your plan assumes, and your carb sensitivity still applies. Practise fuelling on the long walks and agree the hike-day plan with a dietitian.
  • Pack. The pack weights assume a 6 to 8 kg day pack with water, layers and food. Adjust the peak weeks to your actual packed weight.
  • Dates. The plan starts Tuesday 10 Nov 2026, so Week 1 has six days. Week 26 is shown as the week of 3 May 2027. Move it to land right before your climb.
  • Travel. Your Japan trip is placed on 24 Dec to 2 Jan, with no fixed meals or targets on those days.
  • This is not medical advice. Check supplement doses and the calorie target with a doctor or registered dietitian.
